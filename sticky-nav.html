Frequently Asked Questions

Is FastSend free to use?

Yes — creating an account and receiving money is completely free. A small fee only applies to certain outgoing transfers.

Which banks does FastSend support?

We support all major Kenyan banks, plus M-Pesa and Airtel Money for mobile transfers.

How long do transfers take?

Most transfers complete in under 10 seconds. Bank transfers may occasionally take a few minutes during high traffic periods.

Is my money safe with FastSend?

Yes — all transactions are encrypted, and we never store your banking passwords on our servers.

Can businesses use FastSend?

Absolutely. Our API lets businesses integrate FastSend for bulk payouts and automated payments.
